# Bot strategy research v2

The existing ten bot teams now research eight fixed recipes in a separate sandbox. This changes research and its replay, not the account's execution rules, council authority, broker settings, LIVE lock, or stored trades. AI remains disconnected.

## Recipes and selection

The control is the original balanced moving-average displacement recipe, now measured using the same new fill model as every candidate. Comparison recipes include patient trend, quick momentum, selective breakout and wick trend. New families are trend pullback with rejection, breakout/retest with confirmation, and range-edge rejection. The new families use earlier trend efficiency, ATR, price boundaries and completed-candle close location. They reject extreme signal bars and signal gaps. Stops are outside recent structure or an ATR distance, rounded outward to the instrument's simulated tick. They use fixed targets and timeouts, not hindsight-selected exits. Non-control families also require at least 1.2 reward/risk at the modeled fill.

Each deterministic drill has 288 standard synthetic OHLC candles. First 128 candles provide training; the next 64 provide validation. A candidate needs at least three training trades with positive net and two positive validation trades, validation net above the control, and the documented validation drawdown ceiling (the greater of $125 or 1.5 times its training drawdown). Eligible recipes rank on validation net minus half drawdown. Only then is the selected recipe run on the final 96 untouched test candles. If none qualifies, the control remains selected. Warmup reads earlier candles but no position crosses segment boundaries. Selection cannot inspect final test candles.

Six regimes rotate: uptrend, downtrend, sideways range, volatility shock, gap/reversal and quiet/costly market. They are generated by an independent deterministic process with varied wicks, continuous closes/next opens except explicit gaps, and fixed seeds. They are **not real historical data, live data, backtests from screenshots, or realistic models of every market**. This is chronological validation per synthetic drill, not a claim of real-market walk-forward performance. Rule changes require a new research version and new evidence scores.

## Fill and risk model

Signals use completed candles. A pending entry fills at the following candle's open, with one tick of adverse entry slippage. The entry snapshot contains knowledge from the signal candle only. Stops/targets remain levels fixed at the signal. Invalid geometry or insufficient sizing blocks the fill. Stock and micro-contract research budgets are $125; full-size contract budgets are $1,500. These are independent hypothetical budgets, not approval to raise account risk. Stocks cap at 50 shares; futures at three contracts. Futures fees are $4 round trip per contract; stock fees $2 round trip per trade. Tick values/specifications are the existing simulated definitions, not broker-verified live instrument metadata.

OHLC extremes trigger exits. A gap through a stop fills at the worse opening price; losses can exceed the planned stop budget. A target fill assumes its limit price. When both stop and target are touched in one candle, the model conservatively takes the stop first and explicitly records ambiguity. Exit slippage is adverse by one tick. This still cannot resolve actual intrabar sequencing, liquidity, partial fills or exchange execution. Drawdown includes open-position adverse excursions and reserved round-trip fees, using a conservative intrabar assumption rather than inventing the order of high/low. Final segment positions close at the last close; no unfinished trade leaks into another sample.

Cost stress separately reruns the selected recipe and control on the same holdout at **three ticks per side and double fees**. Signals are unchanged, but fill geometry, risk sizing and later availability can change trades or quantity. This is a stressed-policy rerun, not an assertion that the exact same trade list always loses more money.

## Evidence gates, losses and wins

Scores accumulate only for each exact selected recipe/version. The selected-policy totals are labeled as a mixed-policy comparison; they cannot qualify an individual recipe. Research gates require 12 selected rounds, 60 trades, four regimes with at least two selected rounds/trades each, positive net, net above matched control, profit factor at least 1.2, positive cost-stress net above its stressed control, and maximum drill drawdown no worse than control. These thresholds are configurable in code and are research heuristics, not proof of profitability. Samples from independent generated drills are not one continuous portfolio, so maximum drill drawdown is not portfolio drawdown. All synthetic gates can pass while real-market validation remains permanently unmet until genuine OHLC research is added. No automatic promotion occurs.

The bot review queue counts stops/gaps, weak follow-through/time exits, gross wins reversed by costs, net target wins and ambiguous bars. It suggests hypotheses for future independent tests. It does not pretend to know a definitive market cause of a loss, train a neural network, or rewrite strategy rules after every outcome. More wins alone do not establish improvement. The playbook and replay expose entries, stops, targets, rule reasons, quantity, fees, net result and signal timestamps.

## Saving, reopening and running

Open the city training building or **Training lab** from an office/council/vault. Pause and **Run one drill** to inspect one round. Choose the team, expand selection scores/playbook, inspect evidence gates and review queue, then play or scrub the chart. Only revealed candles influence replay scaling; trade exits stay hidden until their candle. Uncheck **Follow newest drill** to freeze a completed replay while background research continues. **Export research** includes original records and scores.

The same browser storage key and export format are retained. Older lifetime counters/history remain; an explicit legacy summary preserves earlier model totals. New comparison scores start fresh for v2 rather than mixing fills from the old model. Existing account storage is never read or changed by the research engine. The lab runs while the app page is open and visible, including outside market hours; hidden/closed pages stop work. Continuous unattended training needs a hosted worker. Broker/market data and AI are still disconnected. Imported historical OHLC, independent forward paper tests and broker-specific cost calibration are future requirements before a claim of strategy improvement.
