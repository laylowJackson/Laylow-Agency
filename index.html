Practice rules and fill assumptions

Practice limits: 4 open positions, $250 planned risk per entry, $1,000 account loss brake and $300 closed-profit brake per team. Stops and targets fill at generated candle closes, so losses can exceed planned risk. Fictional collateral: full stock notional; $5,000 per full-size futures contract or $500 per micro contract. $1 per stock fill, $2 per futures contract per fill, plus one tick slippage each side. These assumptions do not represent a broker's margin or fee schedule.
